## Single Most Important Takeaway

The codebase splits cleanly into two very different stories, and conflating them is the single biggest interview risk: **(1) a strong, real, well-documented AI product engineering story** — structured-output LLM pipelines, fail-closed error handling, deliberate multi-cache strategy, and a real architectural pivot that shipped — which maps well onto the Oneleet JD almost everywhere; and **(2) a strong, real, well-documented Azure *platform* infrastructure story** — Container Apps, private-VNet Postgres, Blob Storage, Entra ID, OIDC CI/CD — that is completely disjoint from Azure's *AI* product line, which is what the Azure Senior AI Engineer JD is actually testing for. The AI backing every pipeline is Google Gemini, never Azure OpenAI/AI Foundry/AI Search/Document Intelligence. The highest-leverage single fix before interviewing for the Azure role is closing that specific gap (port one pipeline to Azure OpenAI); the highest-leverage fix before the Oneleet role is being ready to speak honestly to the two clean, checkable gaps a sharp interviewer will find in minutes — no AI-feature usage analytics, and no shared reusable Gemini-call helper despite five near-identical call sites.
